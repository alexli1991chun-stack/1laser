A FEW GOOD QUESTIONS

Before you
make your move.

Which laser should I choose for my business?

Start with the material, whether you need cutting or engraving, and the largest item you plan to process. Use our shortlist tool, then confirm work area, fixtures, budget and configuration with the team.

Can I engrave metal with these machines?

Coated-metal surface engraving is different from marking or cutting bare metal. Results depend on the laser source, coating and process. Share your exact material and intended result with OneLaser before selecting a machine.

What else do I need besides the laser?

Plan for suitable extraction or ventilation, workspace and power, compatible software, stock for testing and any application-specific fixtures or rotary accessories. Confirm requirements for your model and location.

How many products can I make per hour?

Output depends on the design, material, settings, loading, finishing and inspection. Ask for a sample evaluation using your project. Maximum machine speed alone does not establish order throughput.

Can I use these examples to estimate my earnings?

The gallery shows product ideas, not income forecasts. Your results depend on demand, selling prices, material and labor costs, waste, fees and other business expenses.

Can someone help me get started?

Yes. Explore the sales consultation and one-on-one training links above to learn about available help, scheduling and any applicable fees.
